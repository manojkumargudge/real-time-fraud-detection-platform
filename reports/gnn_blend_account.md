# GNN + XGBoost blend

`(1 - w) * xgb + w * gnn`, generated by `python -m src.graph.blend --graph account` from `artifacts/gnn-account/scores.parquet` (sha256 `9a70d6a68223f265…`).

**Selection (never validation):** the last training block (days 90-141), with out-of-fold inputs only: XGBoost (v2 recipe, seed 42) trained on the earlier blocks (314,954 rows); the transaction score from the GNN trained on the earlier blocks; the entity score = mean of the uid's block-1 scores. Rule: the largest `w` in 0-0.5 whose paired day-block bootstrap (1,000 resamples) 95% CI lower bound of AP(blend) - AP(xgb) is >= -0.01. **Validation** is evaluated once, at the chosen `w` (XGBoost trained on all training rows; entity score from blocks 1-2).

## Limitations

- **Transaction variant, validation scores:** produced by the final GNN on the full graph, so earlier validation transactions are message-passing neighbours (features only, no labels). Temporal sampling keeps every neighbour strictly earlier. This is what an online GNN would see, but it is not a training-only graph, and serving it would need torch and a live graph in the scorer.
- **Selection block vs validation:** selection uses GNNs and XGBoost trained on two thirds of the training window; validation uses models trained on all of it. A weight that suits the weaker selection models need not suit the final ones.
- **Entity variant:** a static uid average, frozen at the end of the history window; most uids have few scored transactions behind it, and block 0 has no scores by design.
- **Rule (flawed):** "largest w not worse by more than the margin" does not pick the best `w` and can pick a `w` that adds nothing or even loses on the selection block; the verdict line says whether there is evidence of a gain.
- One GNN seed (42) and one XGBoost seed (42); the stacking report shows a seed spread of about 0.01 PR-AUC for XGBoost alone.
